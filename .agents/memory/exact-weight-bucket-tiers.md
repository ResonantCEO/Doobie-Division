---
name: Exact weight-bucket tiers
description: Rules for when combined weight options may receive a higher pricing tier.
---

Weight-bucket pricing may promote items only when a combination exactly forms a named tier. Partial combinations retain each item's own tier: 1/4 oz plus 1/8 oz stays at quarter and eighth pricing, while two 1/4 oz items become one exact 1/2 oz bucket and both receive half-ounce pricing.

**Why:** Assigning a partial bucket to the highest threshold it crosses incorrectly discounts smaller items even though the purchase did not actually reach the next offered package tier.

**How to apply:** Form exact 1 oz, 1/2 oz, 1/4 oz, and 1/8 oz groups from largest to smallest. Price leftover units at their original tier. Cart display, totals, promotions, and checkout serialization must use the same allocation.
