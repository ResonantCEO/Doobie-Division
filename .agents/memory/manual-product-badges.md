---
name: Manual product badges
description: How editor-selected product labels relate to actual promotions
---

Manual product badges are visual storefront labels only. Even labels such as Daily Deals and Clearance must not activate pricing changes, schedule discounts, or enroll the product in a daily-deal promotion.

**Why:** The user requested checkboxes for labels similar in appearance to automatic BOGO and stock indicators, but explicitly wanted them manually triggered.

**How to apply:** Keep these selections independent of discount calculation and expiration paths; show only the badges the editor selected.
