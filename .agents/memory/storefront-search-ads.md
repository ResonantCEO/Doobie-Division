---
name: Storefront search advertisements
description: Which advertisements should be hidden during product searches
---

Hide all advertisements assigned inside categories during a product search, even when their category contains matching products. Restore them when the search is cleared. Do not change ordinary category browsing or administrative layout editing.

**Why:** The user wants product searches without category banners, not just banners filtered to matching categories.

**How to apply:** Keep this search rule separate from heading visibility settings and preserve existing restrictions on unassigned advertisements.
