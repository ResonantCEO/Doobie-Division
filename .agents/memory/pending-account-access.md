---
name: Support-only account access
description: Pending and suspended accounts may sign in for support without other account access
---

Pending accounts may log in, but have access only to their own support tickets until approved. This includes creating tickets, exchanging replies and attachments, requesting closure, and signing out. Signing in must never itself activate the account.

Suspended accounts may also log in, but only access their own support tickets until reinstated. Signing in must never lift the suspension.

**Why:** The user wants pending and suspended accounts to be able to contact support.

**How to apply:** Preserve the support-only boundary when editing authentication, public catalog access, navigation, or background connections. Keep inactive and rejected accounts blocked. Approval or reinstatement should restore ordinary access without requiring another login.
