---
name: Pending account access
description: The user's approval policy allows pending accounts to contact support without shopping access
---

Pending accounts may log in, but have access only to their own support tickets until approved. This includes creating tickets, exchanging replies and attachments, requesting closure, and signing out. Signing in must never itself activate the account.

**Why:** The user wants people awaiting account approval to be able to contact support.

**How to apply:** Preserve the support-only boundary when editing authentication, public catalog access, navigation, or background connections. Keep inactive and rejected accounts blocked. Approval should restore ordinary access without requiring another login.
